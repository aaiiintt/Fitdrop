Fitdrop is a personal exploration of fashion from 1980 to 2025.

If you want to know more about an outfit drag it to the corner. If the pile gets too messy restart the drop, or just chuck things around.

The Stack:

  • Gemini 3.0 Pro: Vibe coding and development of specific outfit prompting.
  • Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3.0 Pro Image): Image generation.
  • Matter.js: 2D Physics engine.
  • Python: Auto image cutouts.
  1. I'd been using Nano Banana for a project and found a way of prompting very specific fashion looks.
  2. Wondered what would happen if I built a Fashion Expert 'agent' that could feed this type of image prompt (Answer: you get lots of good images).
  3. I love physics engines (like matter.js) but have never been able to wrangle them. (Now I can, thanks Gemini).
  4. One of my new years resolutions was to 'put myself out there more'. I guess this 45x'es my target.

(And dropping things is just satisfying.)
