Fitdrop was a project to see if I could put myself in iconic looks from 1980-2025.

It was an experiment in two things:

  1. Seeing just how good Nano Banana is at specific clothing looks (answer: I'm impressed, are you?)
  2. How quickly I could vibe-code a fun way to present all these looks (answer: shockingly quickly).

Built with Gemini, Antigravity, Nano Banana, matter.js, and Python.
